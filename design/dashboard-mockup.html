While you were away. Bitcoin does not observe weekends. It is up 3.1% since Friday's close on continued ETF inflows, and three of you are holding it. Those marks are live on your dashboard right now — but nothing settles until Monday's bell.

Worth knowing. Nvidia reports Thursday and is the only item on next week's calendar capable of moving the whole tape. Costco's August comps land Wednesday. Jackson Hole runs Thursday through Saturday, historically a richer source of noise than signal.

What to look at

  • The bell line. Every curve stops at a dashed brass rule with the day beyond it hatched out. The chart says what the game is: nothing is settled until tomorrow's open. The countdown in the header is live and computes the real next open in Eastern time, skipping weekends and market holidays.
  • MERIDIAN is drawn, not labelled. The AI's equity curve is the dashed grey line — non-human is readable from form before you reach the legend. Its journal shows both states at once: Monday's orders sealed, Friday's reasoning published and already being argued with.
  • The weekend split. Equity rows show Friday's close, greyed and tagged. Crypto rows show live marks with a pulsing dot. Your position moves all weekend; you still can't act until Monday.
  • 30-day is the headline. All-time sits underneath it in both the stat strip and the leaderboard, so a family member down over the year can still be having the best month.
  • The 25% cap is shown, not just enforced. Each weight bar carries a brass tick at the cap — NVDA sits just under it. The order ticket surfaces post-fill weight before you commit.
  • Research sits in two places, on purpose. The standing list lives in the rail, but the links that matter are the ones under the brief — you get curious about Nvidia while reading about Nvidia, not an hour later. Every ticker in your positions is itself a link out. All ten sources are free and none need an account.
